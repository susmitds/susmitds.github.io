Questions I keep coming back to

When should a model think explicitly?

Reasoning is useful, but it is also expensive and often deployed as a global mode. I’m interested in models that can allocate explicit reasoning when context actually calls for it.

When is an answer worth relying on?

A rationale can be fluent, faithful, or mechanistically interesting and still fail to justify its conclusion. I’m interested in outputs whose assumptions, evidence and inferential steps can be inspected and challenged.
